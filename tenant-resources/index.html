Tenant Resources

Innago is where you pay rent, sign the lease, request repairs, and exchange messages. This page is the rest: how to get into Innago, and the checklists and contacts Innago does not hold. Sign in to Innago

Mac invites you. You cannot create an Innago account for this home until that email arrives.

  1. Watch for an email invitation from Innago. Open it and accept the invite. Innago will ask you to set a password. The email address on the invite is your username.
  2. If the message is not in your inbox, check spam or junk.
  3. After the account is set up, sign in on the web with the button below, or use the Innago app. Use the same email and password each time.
  4. If sign-in still fails, use the help link on the Innago sign-in page, or email Mac at rollwithdfw@gmail.com.

What you do in Innago

  • Pay rent. Rent is paid online through Innago by bank transfer (ACH). You can turn on autopay in Innago if you want payments to run on a schedule you set there. Your lease states the amount and the day rent is due. [Owner to confirm] the due date to show on this page.
  • View and sign the lease. When a lease is ready, open it from the invite email or from inside Innago, then sign it there.
  • Request maintenance and add photos. Routine repairs such as HVAC, plumbing, or appliances go through Innago, not the emergency line.
  • Messages. Read and send messages with Mac in Innago.
  • Documents. Open the lease and other files shared with you in Innago.

Bank details are entered only in Innago. This website does not take payment and stores no login or payment data.

Move-in checklist

Innago does not store this checklist. These are general steps, plus items Mac still needs to fill in.

  • Accept the Innago invite, sign in, and sign the lease in Innago.
  • Photograph each room, including wear that is already there, and keep the photos with the date. This is general guidance, not a rule from the lease.
  • Note anything that needs repair. Send non-urgent items through Innago.

Move-out checklist

Notice, cleaning, and the deposit follow your lease. This list does not replace it.

  • Email Mac at rollwithdfw@gmail.com when you plan to move out, and give notice the way your lease requires. [Owner to confirm] the notice period and how notice must be delivered.
  • Remove your belongings and trash. [Owner to confirm] the cleaning standard.
  • Return every key and access device. [Owner to confirm] where and when to return them.
  • Send Mac a forwarding address. [Owner to confirm] how the security deposit is returned. No timeline is stated on this site.
  • Photograph the home when you leave, and keep the photos. This is general guidance.
  • Send any repair that is still the landlord’s responsibility through Innago before you go, if your lease tells you to report it that way.
