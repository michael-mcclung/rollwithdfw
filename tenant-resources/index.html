Innago is where you pay rent, sign the lease, request repairs, and exchange messages. This page is the rest: how to get into Innago, and the checklists and house rules Innago does not hold. Sign in to Innago

Mac invites you. You cannot create an Innago account for this home until that email arrives.

  1. Watch for an email invitation from Innago. Open it and accept the invite. Innago will ask you to set a password. The email address on the invite is your username.
  2. If the message is not in your inbox, check spam or junk.
  3. After the account is set up, sign in on the web with the button below, or use the Innago app. Use the same email and password each time.
  4. If sign-in still fails, use the help link on the Innago sign-in page, or email Mac at rollwithdfw@gmail.com.

What you do in Innago

  • Pay rent. Rent is due on the 1st of each month and is paid through Innago, by bank transfer (ACH). You can turn on autopay in Innago. The amount is in your lease and in Innago, not on this page.
  • View and sign the lease. When a lease is ready, open it from the invite email or from inside Innago, then sign it there.
  • Request maintenance and add photos. Send routine repair requests in writing in Innago, or by email. Urgent problems use the phone number in Emergency contacts.
  • Messages. Read and send messages with Mac in Innago.
  • Documents. Open the lease and other files shared with you in Innago.

Bank details are entered only in Innago. This website does not take payment and stores no login or payment data.

Move-in checklist

Your signed lease controls these steps. Innago does not store the checklist.

  • Accept the Innago invite, sign in, and sign the lease in Innago.
  • Within 7 days of move-in, fill out the move-in condition checklist and return it. Include date-stamped photos of each room and of wear that is already there. Still report anything that needs prompt attention, even if it is on the checklist.
  • Keys, remotes, and other access devices are listed on the move-in key receipt when you receive them. Do not copy a controlled access device. Report a lost key right away.
  • The lease does not set a lockbox code or a meeting time. Email rollwithdfw@gmail.com or message Mac in Innago to arrange key pickup.
  • You set up and pay the utilities and services: electric, gas if the home has it, water, sewer, trash, internet, and phone. The owner pays the Ring camera account. Do not let a utility lapse in a way that can damage the home.
  • Get renters insurance before you move in, and keep it for the whole lease. The lease requires at least $300,000 of liability coverage and $20,000 of personal property coverage. Send proof before move-in and again at each renewal. Ask the insurer to list the landlord as an interested party for policy-status notices. That listing does not make the landlord an insured on your policy. Tell Mac if the policy is cancelled or not renewed. The landlord’s property insurance does not cover your belongings.
  • Do not disable smoke or carbon monoxide alarms. Test them, and report a malfunction right away.
  • Save the emergency repair line in your phone: (214) 639‑4141.

Move-out checklist

Give notice, clean, and handle the deposit the way your signed lease describes. This list does not replace it.

  • Give written notice before you leave. Send it by email to rollwithdfw@gmail.com or by Innago message. A month-to-month tenancy needs at least 30 days’ written notice. A fixed term ends as the lease states.
  • Remove your belongings and trash. Do not leave property behind.
  • Clean the home back to its move-in condition, apart from normal wear and tear.
  • Return every key, remote, and access credential. The lease does not name a drop-off spot. Email Mac or message him in Innago to arrange the return.
  • Give Mac a forwarding address in writing. The deposit is returned, or you receive an itemized list of deductions, within 30 days after move-out, once that address is provided. This page does not list the deposit amount. Your signed lease controls what may be deducted.
  • Photograph the home when you leave and keep the photos. Move-out photos are for your records. The required photos are the date-stamped ones with the move-in checklist.
  • Send any repair request in writing through Innago, or by email, before you go.

House rules

These are the lease rules in plain language. Your signed lease controls if a line here ever differs from it.

Noise

Do not create a nuisance, make excessive noise, or disturb neighbors. You are also responsible for your guests. The lease does not set quiet hours. Be considerate, especially in the late evening.

Smoking and vaping

Smoking and vaping are not allowed inside the property.

Pets

No pets without prior written consent. An assistance animal is allowed when fair-housing law requires it, and no pet fee is charged for that animal. You are responsible for damage or conduct related to an animal, as the law allows.

Guests

A guest who stays more than 30 days in a row, or 30 days in any 30-day period, needs written approval first. The home is for private residential use. Do not sublet, assign the lease, or offer a short-term rental without written consent.

Parking

Park in the driveway and the garage only. Do not park on the lawn, block a sidewalk, store a vehicle that does not run, or do major vehicle repairs at the property.

Changes to the home

Do not repaint, change locks, install major equipment, drill significant holes, change the landscaping, or make structural changes without prior written approval. Ask in writing by emailing rollwithdfw@gmail.com or by sending an Innago message.

Care of the home

  • Mow about every two weeks when weather allows. Report irrigation or landscaping problems, and do not damage the lawn.
  • Replace ordinary light bulbs and HVAC filters when asked. Use appliances and drains carefully.
  • Tell Mac promptly about leaks, pests, moisture, or other damage.
  • Send repair requests in writing: an Innago maintenance request, or email to rollwithdfw@gmail.com. Call (214) 639‑4141 for an urgent repair.
  • Do not disable smoke or carbon monoxide alarms. Report a malfunction right away.

Trash and recycling

Trash and recycling follow City of Garland rules. The lease does not print a pickup weekday. Look up your day and your recycling week before you rely on a guess.

  • Trash (green cart) is collected every week. Recycling (blue cart) is collected every other week. Bulky items and brush are collected on your trash day.
  • Find your pickup day and recycling week on MyGarland (opens in a new tab). Questions go to City of Garland Sanitation, (972) 205‑3500, or garlandtx.gov (opens in a new tab). City schedules can change, including on holidays. The City’s current rules control.
  • Set carts out no earlier than 6 p.m. the day before collection, and by 7 a.m. on collection day. Leave them out until at least 7 p.m. Bring them back out of public view by 11 p.m. on collection day.
  • Set bulky items out no earlier than 6 p.m. the day before collection, between the sidewalk and the curb. Do not mix bulky items with brush.
  • Tree limbs may be set out any day. Keep brush separate from bulky items, and keep piles at least 4 feet apart.
  • Do not block a mailbox, a utility, or a driver’s sightline. If the City rejects a set-out, fix it and bring the cart back in.
