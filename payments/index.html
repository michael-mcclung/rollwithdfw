Paying rent

Innago (primary)

Rent is paid through Innago, an online rent platform. After your lease is signed you'll get an email invitation from Innago to set up your tenant account. Accept it, then pay by bank transfer (ACH) inside Innago.

Open Innago

Your bank details are entered only in Innago, never on this website. Innago charges a processing fee for each payment, paid by the tenant. Innago shows the current fee before you pay.

Zelle (optional)

Zelle is available only with payment instructions you've confirmed directly with your landlord. This website never lists Zelle recipient details.

Protect yourself: if you get an email, text or call with new or changed payment instructions, don't send money. Confirm first by calling (214) 639‑4141 or emailing rollwithdfw@gmail.com.

Cash

Cash is accepted only when handed to your landlord, and you'll get a written receipt for every cash payment. Never leave cash unattended or give it to anyone else.

Rent can be paid only through Innago, Zelle or cash. Card and check aren't accepted.

Your signed lease controls due dates, amounts and late fees.
