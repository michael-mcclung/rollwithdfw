FAQ

How do I pay rent?

Rent is paid online through Innago by bank transfer (ACH). Use the Pay Rent button to open Innago’s tenant login.

How do I request a routine repair?

Use Request a Repair to open Innago and submit your request there. That is the path for routine repairs such as HVAC, plumbing, or appliances.

When should I call instead of using Innago?

Call the emergency repair line for urgent issues listed above. For gas smell or fire, call 911 first, then the emergency line if it is safe to do so.

How do I contact Mac?

Email Mac at rollwithdfw@gmail.com using the Email Mac button, or that address directly.
