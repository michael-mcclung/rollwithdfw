FAQ

How do I pay rent?

Rent is paid online through Innago by bank transfer (ACH). Use Portal Login to open Innago. Tenant Resources has the details.

How do I request a routine repair?

Use Portal Login and submit the request in Innago. That is the path for routine repairs such as HVAC, plumbing, or appliances.

When should I call instead of using Innago?

Call the emergency repair line for urgent issues listed above. For gas smell or fire, call 911 first, then the emergency line if it is safe to do so.

How do I contact Mac?

Email Mac at rollwithdfw@gmail.com.

How do I sign in to Innago?

Accept the invitation Innago emails you, then use Portal Login to sign in on the web, or open the Innago app. Tenant Resources walks through the steps.
