Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Set to the date this page goes live]

Who we are

Roll With DFW is an assumed business name (DBA) of Michael McClung. It is not a corporation or LLC. This policy covers www.rollwithdfw.com only. Your lease and any notices required by law are separate.

Contact: rollwithdfw@gmail.com, (214) 639-4141.

Information this website collects

  • Maintenance requests. When you use the form on the Maintenance page, we receive your name, phone number, email (optional), property address and unit, the urgency you pick, your description, your entry preference, and an optional photo.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive whatever you include in that email.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider receives standard request data, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts. It does not collect payment, banking or login information.

How we use it

We use maintenance requests and emails to respond to you, schedule and complete repairs, keep records about the property, and meet our obligations as a landlord. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who receives it

  • Netlify hosts this website and stores maintenance form submissions and photos.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers form notifications and email to the landlord's inbox.
  • Repair contractors may receive the details they need to complete a repair, such as the address, the problem and your contact information.
  • Others when required. We may disclose information when required by law, or to protect people or property in an emergency.

Rent payments happen in Innago and, if you choose, through your bank's Zelle service. Those companies' own privacy policies apply to information you give them.

How long we keep it

We keep maintenance requests and photos while they're needed for property records. We may keep records longer when the law or an open dispute requires it.

Sensitive information

This website never asks for Social Security numbers, driver's license or other government ID numbers, or bank, card or other financial account numbers. If any of that information reaches us through the form or by email anyway, we'll securely delete it as soon as we find it and won't keep it, consistent with Texas Business and Commerce Code §521.052.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS and uses security headers that block framing and third-party content. Access to submissions is limited to the landlord's accounts. No method is perfectly secure, so please don't send bank details, passwords or ID numbers through the form or by email.

Questions

Questions about your information? Email rollwithdfw@gmail.com. We'll reply within one week.

Children

This website is meant for adult tenants and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
