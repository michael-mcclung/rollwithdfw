Emergencies come first

  • Fire, a gas smell, or immediate danger: call 911 first.
  • Leave the unsafe area.
  • Only when you are safe, call the emergency repair line: 469‑783‑5078
  • Emergencies listed in your lease go to the emergency repair line, after 911 when there is danger: fire, flooding, gas smell, sewage backup, no heat in freezing weather, or no AC in extreme heat.
  • Ordinary, non-urgent repairs go through the form on this page.

Do not use this form for emergencies. Submissions are not monitored around the clock.
